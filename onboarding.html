The Four-Step Journey

How Every New Client Moves Through the Centre

01
Minutes 0 -- 25 -- The Hook

The Blood Test & the Wait Window

The client walks in. Concierge intake, complete medical history, signed consent. A licensed nurse takes a single combined finger-prick + venous draw. All four point-of-care machines fire simultaneously: Abbott Afinion 2 (metabolic, lipids, HbA1c, CRP), PixCell HemoScreen (full CBC, 25 parameters), Piccolo Xpress (22 chemistry tests covering liver, kidney, electrolytes), iChroma II (full hormone panel including testosterone, cortisol, TSH, FT3, FT4). Results are ready in 14 minutes. The client never leaves the building.

While the machines run, the client picks one wait-time activity. Three paths, three different conversion psychologies:

Free path

Compression therapy

30-min NormaTec session. Client reclines, completely passive. Cost to us: EUR 5. Perceived value: EUR 55.
Upsell path

Red light therapy or sauna

20-min red light booth or 30-min sauna session. Add-on: EUR 25-45. Margin: 90%+.
Discovery path

InBody + AI skin scan

Body composition + skin biological age in 8 minutes. Adds two datasets to the consult. No extra cost.
02
Minutes 25 -- 50 -- The Wow Moment

Results Consultation with the Medical Director

The client moves into the consultation room, slightly endorphined from compression or red light, holding the welcome drink we offered them at intake. The Medical Director already has every result on screen. 51+ biomarkers + the full hormone panel, all from the same draw, all ready before the client even sat down.

Together they review the data. Deficiencies are circled in real time. Excesses flagged. The doctor explains what each marker means in plain language -- vitamin D, ferritin, hs-CRP, testosterone, cortisol, HbA1c -- and what those values mean for the client's energy, sleep, recovery and long-term risk.

Then the doctor turns the screen and opens the Biohacking Studio app on the client's own phone via QR code. Their data is already in it. Their personalised protocol is already drafted. Their biological age is already calculated. This is the moment that converts -- the prospect is no longer being sold a membership, they are being shown their own future.

03
Minutes 50 -- 65 -- The Take-Home

The Supplement Protocol -- Minimum Effective Dose

Most longevity clinics push fifteen pills a day. We do the opposite. The Medical Director prescribes only what the bloodwork actually justifies -- typically 3 to 5 supplements, never more than 7 unless the data demands it. This is the differentiator vs HUM2N, Biowell, every IV-drip clinic in London. We minimise, not maximise.

The protocol is sourced through one of two channels: our partnership with BE LEVELS (Spain's premier supplement brand) for retail-grade fulfilment, or the white-label Biohacking Studio house line for higher-margin members. Both are in the app. Both have one-tap reorder. Both ship to the client's home before they run out.

A
Doctor selects from a curated list of 24 longevity-grade compounds based on the panel: e.g. low ferritin → iron bisglycinate; flat HRV → magnesium glycinate + ashwagandha; elevated CRP → omega-3 + curcumin; low D → D3+K2.
B
App auto-loads the stack with timing, dosage, food/fasted instructions, and a 30-day supply countdown. Push notifications at the right time of day for each compound.
C
Quarterly re-test shows whether the deficiency moved. If it didn't, we change the compound or the dose. If it did, we de-escalate. The protocol shrinks as the client improves.
04
Minutes 65 -- 90 -- The Close

Personalising the Membership

The three tiers come up on the consult screen with the client's data already mapped to each one. The Seeker covers their basics; The Vitalist gives them the hormone panel and four IVs they actually need; The Architect adds NAD+ if their HRV and biomarkers justify it. The client picks based on budget and time. The Medical Director then mixes and matches the included services to fit their actual results -- swap red light for compression, swap one IV for two skin scans, swap NAD+ for peptide therapy.

Payment terminal at the consult desk. They pay. The blood test bill and the wait-time activity bill are wiped from the receipt. They walk out a member, with the app on their phone, the supplement order placed, and the next quarterly re-test already booked.

Pre-Launch Demand Engine

The Early Access Waitlist + Referral Programme

Borrowed from Neko Health's London playbook. The app launches publicly in Month 2 -- four months before the centre opens. Anyone in Barcelona can join the waitlist. Every five referrals jumps the referrer up the priority queue. By Grand Opening, we have a pre-validated demand pipeline and a self-funded acquisition channel.

01

Public app launch -- Month 2

The Biohacking Studio app goes live on iOS and Android with a single screen: "Join the waitlist for Barcelona's first medical longevity club. Opening Month 4." No price tag, no commitment. Just an email, a phone number, and a postcode.

02

The referral mechanic

Each waitlister gets a unique invite code. Refer 5 friends who also join the waitlist, and you skip ahead 4 weeks. Refer 10, you become a Founding 100 member with locked-in pricing. Refer 20, you get your first month free at any tier. Pure organic acquisition.

03

Founding 100 priority

The first 100 to convert from waitlist to paid get permanent founding-member rates (locked at the tier price they signed at, for life). They get their name on the lounge wall. They get the first 100 blood test slots in launch month. They become the social proof for everyone after them.

04

What this gives the investor

By Day 120, we expect 600-1,000 waitlist signups in Barcelona, with 100-150 paid memberships pre-converted before the doors open. Year 1 MRR is no longer a hope -- it is a pre-sold pipeline that materially de-risks the membership ramp.
